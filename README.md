## 🌱 About the Project

**Farmer App** is a technology-driven agricultural application designed to help farmers access useful digital services and agricultural information through a **simple, accessible, and farmer-friendly platform**.

The project is developed as part of the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) — Problem Statement 26033**, with the goal of using technology to address real-world challenges faced by the farming community.

> 🌾 **Our vision:** Make useful agricultural technology easier to access, easier to understand, and easier to use — especially for the farmers who need it most.

## 💡 Why Farmer App?

Agriculture is more than just a profession — it is the backbone of our society. 🌾

However, farmers can face challenges when trying to access:

* 📚 Reliable agricultural information
* 🌦️ Useful farming-related updates
* 💰 Relevant market information
* 🧑‍🌾 Digital agricultural services
* 📱 Easy-to-use technology
* 🌱 Resources that support better farming decisions

**Farmer App** brings these digital capabilities together in one convenient platform.
