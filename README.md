## 🚨 The Problem

<img align="right" src="https://capsule-render.vercel.app/api?type=transparent&height=140&text=₹%20&fontSize=90&animation=twinkling" width="140"/>

> **Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.**

Every extra middleman in the supply chain quietly eats into a farmer's profit — and inflates what the end consumer pays. The farmer who grows the crop earns the least; the person who never touched the soil often earns the most.

<div align="center">

| 😔 Farmer Sells At | 🏬 Consumer Pays | 💸 Lost In Between |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Low mandi price | High retail price | Multiple middlemen margins |

</div>

## 🌱 Our Solution — AgriDirect

<div align="center">
<img src="https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Poppins&weight=600&size=20&duration=2200&pause=800&color=43A047&center=true&vCenter=true&width=650&lines=A+Direct+Farmer-to-Consumer+Marketplace;No+Middlemen.+No+Guesswork.+Just+Fair+Trade." />
</div>

**AgriDirect** connects verified farmers directly with verified buyers — cutting out unnecessary intermediaries while still giving farmers live market intelligence to price fairly and buyers confidence to purchase safely.
